The C64 is famous for its SID chip.

The computer museum likely has several.
As does the retro computing group at the Makerspace.

The incantation to launch the VICE emulator is x64sc!

I nabbed this from c64-wiki.com/wiki/SID

```
 0 rem *** c64-wiki sound-demo ***
 1 rem *** set up jump to one of the following lines randomly
10 s = 54272: w = 17: on int(rnd(ti)*4)+1 goto 12,13,14,15
 2 rem set the waveforms. still haven't worked out the bloody numbers
12 w = 33: goto 15
13 w = 65: goto 15
14 w = 129
15 poke s+24,15: poke s+5,97: poke s+6,200: poke s+4,w
16 for x = 0 to 255 step (rnd(ti)*15)+1
17 poke s,x :poke s+1,255-x
18 for y = 0 to 33: next y,x
19 for x = 0 to 200: next: poke s+24,0
20 for x = 0 to 100: next: goto 10
21 rem *** abort only with run/stop ! ***
```
